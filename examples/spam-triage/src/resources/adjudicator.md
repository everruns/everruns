Judge the message itself, not the tone. Unsolicited bulk advertising, chain
letters, adult or medical offers, get-rich schemes, and forged senders after
money or credentials are SPAM. Correspondence, mailing lists and newsletters the
recipient subscribed to, receipts, and automated notices from services they use
are LEGITIMATE — a marketing voice, an HTML layout, or an unsubscribe link does
not by itself make a message spam.

Answer with exactly one word, in capitals: SPAM or LEGITIMATE. No punctuation,
no explanation.
