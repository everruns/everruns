## Durable Agent Sessions — Brief

**Finding 1: Emerging infrastructure layer.** Durable Sessions are described as a
persistent, addressable connection between agents and users that outlives any single
connection, device, or process crash — covering stream resumption, crash detection, and
cross-device continuity.
Source: https://ably.com/blog/durable-sessions-infrastructure-layer-ai-agents

**Finding 2: Three-stack formation.** The ecosystem is forming around three layers:
Durable Streams (replayable protocol primitive, open-sourced by ElectricSQL), Durable
Sessions (the user-agent session layer), and Durable Execution (backend workflow engines
like Temporal that persist state at each step for automatic retry/resumption).
Sources:
https://ably.com/blog/durable-ai-infrastructure-sessions-streams-transports-ecosystem ·
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/durable-task/sdks/durable-task-for-ai-agents

**Finding 3: Session persistence ≠ durable execution.** Persisting conversation history
(e.g., OpenAI Agents SDK with SQLite/Redis backends) provides continuity but does not
guarantee crash-safe workflow replay; full durability also requires checkpointing
execution state, idempotency keys, and deterministic replay.
Source: https://zylos.ai/research/2026-04-24-durable-execution-agent-runtimes/

**Inference:** As production agents span hours or days with human-in-the-loop steps,
durable sessions will likely become a standard middleware category — analogous to message
queues — sitting between agent frameworks and transport layers, commoditizing what teams
currently build ad hoc.
