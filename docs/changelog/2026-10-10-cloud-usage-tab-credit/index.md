**Settings** > **Usage** shows what your organization was charged on
[Everruns Cloud](https://app.everruns.com): per service, per day, per model and
per agent, with input and output tokens next to the dollars, the credit
activity, and a CSV export. Agents that use your own provider keys are not
billed here, and the tab says so.

A credit chip under the sidebar shows the balance and links to **Billing**. A
banner appears on every page when credit is low (under $1), below zero, or used
up, asking members to contact an admin and admins to top up.

See [Credit and the spend cap](/getting-started/cloud/#credit-and-the-spend-cap).
