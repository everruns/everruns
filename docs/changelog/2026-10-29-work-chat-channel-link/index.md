A published **Work Chat** channel page now shows the chat's address. Copy it
from **Settings** or **Connect**, or open it with **Open chat page** in the
header, including while you are editing. The address stays the same when you
change settings. It appears after you publish, on a deployment that has a
hosted chat domain.

See [Work Chat](/features/channels/#work-chat).
