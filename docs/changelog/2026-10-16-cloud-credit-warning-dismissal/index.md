Credit warnings on [Everruns Cloud](https://app.everruns.com) now have a close
button. Dismiss a warning once to keep it hidden for your account and
organization across reloads, browsers, and devices. Other members keep their
own preferences, and a different credit warning state still appears.

![The exhausted-credit warning with a close button beside the provider-key link](./credit-warning@2x.png)

The sidebar credit balance stays visible after dismissal, with a link to
Billing. See [Credit and the spend cap](/getting-started/cloud/#credit-and-the-spend-cap)
for how built-in models use your organization's credit.
