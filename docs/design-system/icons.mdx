## Two families

- **Facet** draws Everruns' own domain entities: agents, harnesses, sessions, models,
  capabilities, sandboxes, and the rest. One glyph per domain, used everywhere that domain
  appears: navigation, Settings, search, page mastheads, and as the fallback image of a record.
- **Lucide** draws conventional actions, directions, and states: add, search, edit, close,
  chevrons, alerts. Always with square caps and mitered joins (`.icon-sharp`).

Official third-party marks (MCP, Slack, model providers) keep their own identity and are
shown at native color, small, on white or smoke surfaces. Uploaded agent avatars and icons a
person chose for a harness or capability also keep theirs.

## Facet

The agent mark, **Intent**, is solid: three separated, ascending planes. Every other glyph is a
quiet geometric outline on a 24-unit artboard with a 1.75-unit stroke. Judge a glyph at 16px,
the navigation size, first. The intent and acceptance bar live in
[Facet iconography](https://github.com/everruns/everruns/blob/main/knowledge/ui/iconography.md);
the paths live in [`apps/ui/src/components/icons/facet-icon-data.ts`](https://github.com/everruns/everruns/blob/main/apps/ui/src/components/icons/facet-icon-data.ts).

The catalog below is rendered from those vector masters when this site builds, at 24px and at
the 16px navigation size.

<DsFacetIcons />

Glyphs inherit the color of their surface. Selection uses the surrounding active state; there
is no icon accent palette.

| Size | Where |
|---|---|
| 12px | Inside badges and chips. |
| 14px | Inline with 12 to 13px text: search fields, detail rows, small buttons. |
| 16px | The default: buttons, navigation, table actions. |
| 20px | Card titles and the glyph inside the 40px masthead tile. |
| 40px | Empty states. |

Icons next to text are decorative and hidden from screen readers. An icon-only button needs an
accessible name on the button.

## Symbols in copy

A small, deliberate set: `⌘` in shortcut hints (`⌘K`), `·` between inline metadata
("12 sessions · 3 channels"), and `→` sparingly in copy, never as a control. No emoji anywhere:
not in the product, the docs, or marketing. Experimental features use the Lucide flask, not
a stamp or an emoji.

## Logo

Three interlocking rings in a navy to gold gradient: durability, scalability, reliability. The
gradient exists only inside the logo.

- Use the color logo on white, smoke, or a plain dark surface, and the mono logo where only one
  color is available. Never clip, recolor, or set it on a busy background or on the dot grid.
- Every logo asset is centered on the rings' centroid, not on its bounding box. The geometry,
  the file list, and the rule for regenerating raster copies live in the
  [brand specification](https://github.com/everruns/everruns/blob/main/knowledge/ui/brand.md).
